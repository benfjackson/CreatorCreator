## Consensus IA sketches

### B2B SaaS (consensus)

```
Home
├── Product (features, platform)
├── Solutions / Use cases (optional)
├── Pricing ★
├── Customers / Case studies
├── Resources (Blog, Docs, Changelog)
├── Company (About, Careers)
└── App (Sign in / Sign up) ★
```

**Outliers:** PostHog puts Docs in primary nav; Vercel leads with product launches.

### B2B services (consensus)

```
Home
├── Services / Capabilities
├── Industries / Who we serve
├── Insights / Case studies ★ (credibility)
├── About / Team
├── Contact / Book intro ★
└── (Pricing hidden or on-page tiers for productised services)
```

**Outliers:** Gong looks like SaaS IA but pricing is gated; FOREsight is single-page.

### Mission-led (consensus)

```
Home
├── Mission / Impact story ★
├── Programmes / What we do
├── Resources / Frameworks ★
├── Get involved (Donate, Partner, Subscribe)
├── News / Events
├── About / Team / Funders
└── (Separate institute or shop if applicable)
```

**Outliers:** Thankyou is commerce-first with mission woven in; Atlassian Foundation is corporate subsite.
